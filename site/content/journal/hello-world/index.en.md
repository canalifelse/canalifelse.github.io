Every program starts with a `Hello, world`. So does this channel.

If Else is a YouTube channel, and this website is the notebook that sits next to it: a home for notes, drafts, and ideas that need a little more room than a video allows.

## Why "If Else"?

Because almost everything we do here starts with a condition:

```js
if (curious) {
  build(); learn(); share();
} else {
  rollForCuriosity();
}
```

**If** a topic sparks our curiosity, we dive in, build something, and share what we learned. **Else**, we roll the dice and see where they take us. Either way, there is always an else.

## What we will talk about

The topics follow four branches:

- **Software development**: building, breaking, debugging, and understanding why it worked (or why it didn't).
- **Data science**: finding the story in the numbers by asking the right questions.
- **Board games**: the six-sided kind of dice, and everything a game night teaches about systems, decisions, and luck.
- **Other things**: everything else falls into the else.

## And the duck?

Programmers know *rubber duck debugging* well: when a problem won't budge, you explain your code, line by line, to a rubber duck. Halfway through, the answer usually shows up on its own.

That is the idea behind the channel. Explaining things out loud, slowly and without skipping steps, is the best way to really understand them. No question is too basic for the duck.

## The dice are cast

The videos live on the [If Else YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@canalifelse), and the posts land here, on this site. If you like learning by doing, asking questions, and the occasional board game, pull up a chair.

When in doubt, ask the duck. Quack!
